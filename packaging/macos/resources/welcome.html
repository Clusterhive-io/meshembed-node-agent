MeshEmbed Node

This installer places the MeshEmbed Node daemon at /usr/local/libexec/meshembed-node and registers a LaunchAgent that starts it at login.

On first start the daemon opens http://127.0.0.1:7842/ in your default browser. Paste your invite token there to enroll this Mac into the MeshEmbed network.

You can get an invite token from your operator dashboard.
